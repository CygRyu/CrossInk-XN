## Save file

`/.crosspoint/cultivation/save.bin`: 1,399 bytes = 10-byte header + 1,389-byte payload, little-endian (save version 5; version 1 had 945 bytes, version 2 949, version 3 1,149 and version 4 1,341, and all load with the newer blocks zeroed).

| Offset | Size | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 10 | magic `PI`, version u16, payload size u16, CRC-32 of payload |
| 10 | 68 | Stats snapshot: seconds, pages, sessions, completed books, 4 time-of-day buckets, 7 weekday buckets, last visit day, seconds credited in that day's week |
| 78 | 16 | Baseline totals at creation (Past Life prologue) |
| 94 | 42 | Character: name (24 B UTF-8), gender, origin, root element and secondary, purity, talent, hidden trait, temperament, 2 paths, karma, rerolls allowed/used, flags, creation day |
| 136 | 38 | Progress: realm, stage, Qi (6 B), stones, stamina, HP, max HP, age in months, age carry, Qi carry, failures, 3 grades, consolidation, Dao Heart, flags (bit 0x04: odd page toward the next stone), reading seconds |
| 174 | 781 | Reserved at full design size: Daos and meridians 23 (v0.2.4: bytes 18-22 hold the 36 opened-point bits, bit i = point i; 0-17 stay for the Daos), scrolls 513, inventory 96 (the v0.1.10 bag; empty since v0.2.0), world 37, timers 16 (v0.2.0: the cauldron and pills), daily 25, rival 11, flags and codex 48 (v0.2.0: bytes 0-4 alchemy), legacy 12 |

No version change |

The bag: v0.1.10 kept it in the reserved 96-byte block (32 slots). v0.2.0 gives every region its own herb and ore and adds pills, 52 item kinds in all, so the bag moved to the 192-byte v4 block (64 slots, one per kind, so it never fills). On the first visit with v0.2.0 the old block is moved over and zeroed: graded herbs and ores (ids 1-6) become the herb or ore of the newest open region of their grade, or the first region of that grade if none is open yet; every other item keeps its id. Item ids (53 Dross since v0.2.1): 7-9 tonics, 10 Thunder-Warding Talisman, 11 Calm-Heart Incense, 12 Region Talisman, 13-22 each region's herb, 23-32 each region's ore, 33-41 breakthrough pills (grade x 3 + quality), 42-50 grade pills (Foundation-Building, Core-Forming, Soul-Clearing x 3 + quality), 51 Qi Gathering pill, 52 recipe slip (merchant stock only, never in the bag).

Alchemy (v0.2.0) uses reserved bytes with no other format change. Timers: byte 0 the brewing recipe + 1 (0 = empty), 2-5 the reading seconds when the brew began, 6-7 brews begun (seeds the quality roll), 8 the breakthrough pill taken for the attempt under way, 9 the pill choice (0 the best that fits, 0xFF none, else an item id), 10-13 Qi Gathering seconds left; 1, 14 and 15 spare (the doc's expedition timer will need its own room). Flags and codex: bytes 0-1 known recipes, 2 grade pills taken toward the next grade, 3 their score, 4 bit 0x01 the Herbalist's starting recipe was given, 5 depth (v0.2.1). Timers byte 1 (v0.2.1): brews in the batch under way (0 = one). Exploration bytes 6 (bit 0x01: the last step brought a random fight) and 7 (the waiting random fight is returning foe N - 1) are used since v0.2.1.

Technique Scrolls (milestone 7) fill the reserved 513-byte block with no format change: byte 0 is the count, then up to 64 slots of 8 bytes in minting order: title hash u32 (never 0), hours read in that book (cap 255; the tier is derived), element (bits 0-2) and kind (bits 3-5), comprehension 0-100, flags (0x01 equipped manual, 0x02 equipped technique). Removing a slot moves the later ones down and zeroes the last. The manual-pity counter has no room there, hence the 4-byte v2 block.

Rules: append-only. A new field goes at the end, `kSaveVersion` and `kPayloadSize` go up, and older saves load with the new field at its default. All-zero must mean "empty" for every reserved byte, so later milestones can give meaning to reserved bytes without a format change. A newer save loads its known prefix in older firmware (and that firmware writes back only what it knows).

Differences from the design document's budget: the stats snapshot is 68 bytes instead of 24, because it also keeps the time-of-day and weekday buckets (needed for affinity and for attributing reading to calendar weeks), and there is a 16-byte baseline block. The doc's 37-byte `world` block could not hold 10 regions of charted and used-up bits, so v0.1.10 appended its own 200-byte block, and v0.2.0 a 192-byte bag; the file is now about 1.35 KB, over the doc's "under 1 KB" (the user was told).

Other files the game keeps in `/.crosspoint/cultivation/`: `chronicle.txt`, `index.bin` and `pack_report.txt` (packs), `pending_scrolls.bin`, `pending_scrolls.rest` and `minted.bin` (scrolls). v0.1.8 also wrote `rumor.txt` for the sleep screen; v0.1.9 no longer shows the rumor there and deletes the file on the next visit. Outside it: `/Chronicles/The Chronicle of <name>.txt`, written only when the player exports the Chronicle.

## Memory

- `CultivationActivity` allocates `GameState` (~1 KB) with `makeUniqueNoThrow` in `onEnter()` and frees it in `onExit()`. If the allocation fails, the game shows an out-of-memory message instead of aborting.
- The keyboard child activity is allocated by the same pattern and owned by `ActivityManager` (existing CrossInk code).
- `renderer.wrappedText` returns a few short strings per render for wrapped notes (render-time only, as CrossInk's other screens do).
- The creation draft (name, up to 9 destiny rolls) is ~60 bytes of members, gone once the activity closes.
- Content packs and the grammar engine, allocated in `onEnter()` after the save loads and freed in `onExit()`: `PackStore` (~1.3 KB: file table, read buffer), its index (384 × 12 B = 4.6 KB, one allocation sized once) and `Grammar` (~3.4 KB: 1 KB output, 1 KB line arena, ~1 KB anti-repeat memory, ~0.4 KB variables). Heap rather than members of the activity so the game still runs (without generated text) if they cannot be allocated. With the ~1 KB save that is about 10.5 KB, inside the design document's 12 KB. On the Return summary the life log adds ~1.6 KB for a peak of about 12 KB; the Chronicle viewer (~0.3 KB since v0.2.3) is never open at the same time, and both are freed when their screen closes.
- A pack rebuild (only after pack files change) borrows ~4.3 KB more for its duration: a 256-byte line buffer, the findings, the first 20 report messages (3.2 KB) and the checker (~0.7 KB). It happens before `Grammar` is allocated, so the freed space is reused right away.
- Return summary: the visit's `LifeLog` (~1.6 KB: 8 lines of 200 bytes) is allocated when a visit with reading starts and freed when the summary is left (or in `onExit()`). If it cannot be allocated, the summary shows the numbers without lines and nothing is added to the Chronicle.
- Chronicle viewer: `ChronicleView` (~0.3 KB since v0.2.3: the starts of up to 64 consecutive pages already laid out, only a cache) is allocated when the Chronicle is opened and freed when it is closed. Each page is read straight from the card, one paragraph at a time, through a 256-byte stack buffer; the searches in `Chronicle.cpp` (line starts, "Age N" headings) read ~300 bytes at a time through the stack. Nothing grows with the file.
- Breakthrough and Great Perfection outcomes keep their Chronicle line (~200 B) and result in members of the activity; no new heap blocks. The no-repeat count walks every season since season 0 (a few thousand cheap steps at most, no memory).
- Writing the Chronicle uses a 256-byte stack buffer for the file's tail; lines are appended, never rewritten.
- Saving streams the payload twice, once to compute the CRC and once to the file, so there is no payload-sized buffer.
- Exploration (v0.1.10): `ExploreView` (~1.7 KB: the open regions' names and charting goals, the open map's layout regenerated from the seed (~80 B), the cursor, the last event's and fight's text, the merchant's stock and the bag's rows) is allocated when Explore is opened and freed when it is left (or in `onExit()`). Never at the same time as the life log, the Chronicle viewer or the library. Drawing the map uses no buffers: icons, fog and crags are drawn pixel by pixel, no bitmaps in flash. The life log grew by two lines (Spatial Upheaval) to ~2 KB.
- Alchemy (v0.2.0): `AlchemyView` (~0.3 KB: known recipes, held ingredients, the experiment's parts, the last result's line) while the Alchemy screens are open; `IngredientNames` (720 B: the world's 20 ingredient names) while Exploration or Alchemy is open. Opening the Bag from Alchemy borrows an `ExploreView` for the Bag's rows and frees it on the way back. Recipes are regenerated from the world seed when needed (a 10-recipe book is ~130 B on the stack), never stored.
- Technique Scrolls (milestone 7), each only while needed: `MintWork` (~0.4 KB: 32 pending books and the hashes minted) while a visit starts, if any book is waiting; `ScrollNews` (~0.8 KB: 8 events with names) from the visit's start until its screen is left; `LibraryView` (~1 KB: the display order of 64 slots and the names of one page, up to 12 rows) while the Techniques screens are open. Never at the same time as the Chronicle viewer; the news can coexist with the life log, for a peak of about 13 KB on the Return summary of a visit that also minted scrolls. Names are generated in the input loop into these buffers, never while drawing. The hook itself streams one 12-byte record; `minted.bin` is read 4 bytes at a time.
- Largest stack users: `readLocalStats()` holds a `GlobalReadingStats` (~160 B) for one call; `save::store`/`load` use two 64-byte path buffers and a 32-byte scratch.

## Stats rules (StatsBridge)

- **Local stats only:** `GlobalReadingStats::load()`, never `loadAggregated()`. CrossInk stats files are never written.
- **Reset detection:** any total going down (seconds, pages, sessions, any time-of-day or weekday bucket) rebases the snapshot and credits nothing. `completedBooks` going down alone is not a reset, because unmarking a book does that. `localResetRevision()` is a RAM counter that restarts at 0 on boot, so it is compared only within one boot (`RevisionWatch`).
- **Weekly allowance** (decision 6A, replaces the doc's daily soft cap): 100% Qi for the first 14 h read in a calendar week, Monday to Sunday, 50% for the next 14 h, 25% after. A calendar week rather than a rolling 7 days, because a rolling window would need 7 per-day totals in the save; the calendar week needs none, and it matches the simulator.
  - The snapshot's `lastVisitDay` stays a day index. Its partner field, renamed `secondsCountedInLastVisitWeek` (same offset and size), now holds the reading already credited in the week containing that day. Save v1 stored "seconds credited on that day" there; read as a week total it can only under-count the week, so old saves need no conversion.
  - Same week as the last visit: the new reading uses what is left of that week's allowance.
  - Later week: CrossInk's per-weekday totals say which weekdays the new reading fell on. Each weekday's seconds are spread evenly over that weekday's dates in the gap; dates in the last visit's week use what was left of it, dates in full weeks in between get a fresh allowance each, and dates in the current week start the new week's count. Rounding remainders and reading CrossInk could not date go to the current week.
  - Without a clock, the whole gap counts as one fresh week; X4 "reading days" come later.
- **Sessions are not used.** Stamina is per 15 minutes read (decision 4A), so CrossInk not storing session lengths no longer matters.

## Resource rules (Simulation)

- **Qi:** 10 per qualifying minute × talent × purity, kept exact with a carry.
- **Age:** 1 month per 5 reading minutes, all minutes (allowance or not).
- **Spirit Stones** (decision 5B): 1 per minute read plus 1 per 2 pages; the page part of one visit is at most half that visit's minutes. One leftover page carries to the next visit (flag bit `kOddPageCarry`); pages over the limit are dropped, so auto page-turns gain nothing. A 0.7 and a 1.4 pages-per-minute reader earn 40 and 45 stones per 30 minutes.
- **Stamina** (decision 4A): +1 per 15 minutes read, nothing per session, bank cap 20 (12 until v0.2.3; user, session 9). A step on the map costs 1, charted or not; a full bank refills in 5 reading hours.
- **Dao Heart** (decision 2B): `100 × reading seconds / 15 h`, capped at 100, never decays. The reading streak becomes flavor and the Dao of Time unlock only.
- **Origin gifts** (agreed in session 2): starting stones and max HP are granted at creation; a character made before gifts existed gets them once on its next visit (character flag `kOriginGiftsGiven`, bit 0x04 of a byte that already exists, so no format change). No origin changes the Qi rate. The other gifts are recorded in `kOriginBonuses` for the systems that will use them.

  | Origin | Stones | Now | Later |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Farmer's child | 30 | +20 max HP (120) | |
  | Fallen clan heir | 100 | | +5 Foundation grade score (milestone 5); rival known from day one |
  | Street orphan | 10 | | +5 luck on event checks (milestone 8) |
  | Sect servant | 50 | | Sect gate open from Qi Condensation instead of Foundation (milestone 8) |
  | Herbalist's apprentice | 40 | | Knows one Qi pill recipe; +10% brew quality (milestone 8) |
- Minutes for stones, steps for stamina and Dao Heart all come from `gameReadingSeconds` (this character's total reading time), so none of them needs its own carry field.

## Content packs and the grammar engine (milestone 4, the doc's build step 3)

Pack format and grammar syntax for authors: `tools/cultivation/PACKS.md`.

- **Sources:** the built-in pack (source 0, flash) and up to 15 SD files from `/.crosspoint/cultivation/lists/*.txt` and `events/*.txt` (sources 1..15, in name order). An SD list with an existing key adds entries; `[!key]` replaces earlier ones.
- **Index:** one `PackSection` (12 B: key hash, byte offset, entry count, source, flags) per `[section]`, not per line. Reading entry i walks the section's lines from its start; lists are short, and reading forward continues from the last entry, so the checker never rewinds.
- **`index.bin`:** the SD sections, plus each file's name, size and modification time and a hash of the built-in pack, with a CRC. Any difference (file added, removed, edited, new firmware pack, damaged cache) triggers a rescan on the device. The built-in pack is re-parsed from flash on every open (a few ms).
- **Checking:** a rescan runs `PackChecker` over the SD packs (built-in lists count as known keys) and writes `pack_report.txt`, or deletes it when all is well. The count is kept in `index.bin`, so the Cultivator's notice survives later opens. The host tests run the same checker on the built-in pack in CI, and `test/cultivation` also builds `cultivation_pack_check` for checking packs on a computer.
- **Engine determinism:** output is a pure function of seed, packs and the anti-repeat history of the open session. Seeds are mixed before use, so consecutive days give unrelated picks.
- **Rumor of the day:** `{rumor}` seeded by `contentSeed(world seed, 50, day index)`; without a clock, by reading hour. Shown under the visit summary.

## Life log and the Chronicle (milestone 5, the doc's build step 4)

- **Seasons:** a season is 3 in-game months, 15 reading minutes. Seasons are counted from `ageMonths` (season k = months 3k..3k+2), so short visits add up without a save field.
- **Lines:** one per completed season, at most 6 per visit; more than 6 are spread evenly, first and last included. Plus one line when stages were gained (`log.stage`) and one when the Peak bar fills (`log.bottleneck`). Each line starts "Spring, age 17." and is seeded by `contentSeed(world seed, 51, season)` (52 stage, 53 bottleneck), so the same season always gives the same line.
- **Lists:** three rolls seeded by the season alone. 30%: `log.any`. Otherwise the realm's list: Body Tempering the origin's own (`log.body.farmer` ... `log.body.herbalist`, 3 in 7) or `log.body`; Qi Condensation `log.qi`; Foundation Establishment `log.foundation`; later realms `log.any`. In Great Perfection `log.gp`, after a failed breakthrough (until the next success) `log.halfstep`, take 35% of the realm-list seasons. Lines never grant anything and never mention Spirit Stones (a host test checks).
- **No early repeats** (milestone 6): each list's position is the number of earlier seasons whose rolls point at its slot (recomputed, never stored), and the list is walked in one fixed shuffled order per world (`(a * i + b) mod n`, `a` coprime to `n`). The rolls never depend on the character's state, so entering Great Perfection, failing a breakthrough or changing realm cannot shift an order: `log.any` never repeats before all its entries are used, realm lists keep at least half that spacing during Great Perfection or Half-step, and those lists repeat only within one stretch of that state. (Two earlier versions, a new shuffle per pass and replayed weighted picks, repeated early; the simulator found both.) `Grammar::expandEntry` forces the top-level pick.
- **Chronicle file:** `/.crosspoint/cultivation/chronicle.txt`. A new character (or the first visit of one made before this update) appends an opening chapter: the book's title if the file is new, "Life N: name", then `chronicle.birth.<origin>`, `chronicle.awakening` and, if there was earlier reading, `chronicle.past`. Each visit appends its lines at the bottom, with an "Age N" heading when the age changes.
- **No double lines:** the Chronicle is written before the save. If the save fails, the next visit re-counts the same reading, builds the same lines, finds the file already ends with them, and skips them. A line cut short by a power loss is closed with a newline before anything new is written.
- **Reading it (v0.2.3, agreed with the user):** the viewer opens on the **newest page**: the fullest page that ends at the end of the file (a binary search over the last ~48 line starts, about 6 page layouts, never a read from the top). **Previous** past the pages already seen lays out the fullest page that ends exactly where the current one starts, the same way, and never starts a page right under its "Age N" heading, so nothing is shown twice, nothing skipped, and headings stay with their entries. The title is the page's age, "Chronicle · age 52" (its first season line or heading, else the nearest heading above), or "Chronicle · birth" on the opening chapter. **Select opens a menu:** Newest page, Beginning, 10 years back, 10 years forward, Export a copy. A jump lands on the "Age N" heading nearest the target in its direction (ages without lines have none); back past the start goes to the beginning, forward past the newest heading to the newest page; a jump that cannot move stays put. Up on the first page and Down on the newest do nothing. The file stays append-only and is never trimmed: about 450 KB for the whole Mortal Plane.
- **Which viewer:** the Cultivator's Chronicle action opens the game's own page viewer. It is not CrossInk's TXT reader, which would record the file as the current book (sleep screen, resume on boot) and return Home on Back. No reading stats are recorded by the viewer. (CrossInk's TXT reader records none either; if a future CrossInk update changes that, `.txt` reading would start earning Qi, which would also matter for the Chronicle only if it were opened there.)

## Breakthroughs, Great Perfection and grades (milestone 6, the doc's build step 5)

## Technique Scrolls (milestone 7, the doc's build step 6)

- **Hook:** marking a book finished or unfinished (in the EPUB or XTC reader, on the book's stats screen, or from the Home/library book menu) appends a 12-byte record to `pending_scrolls.bin`, only if a save exists. Creating a character deletes `pending_scrolls.bin` and `minted.bin`: books finished before this life give nothing. Books finished before this firmware are not known to the game at all.
- **Minting** (each visit, before the save): the records are replayed (an unmark before minting cancels the mark; a later mark refreshes the numbers), up to 32 books per visit; the rest wait in `pending_scrolls.rest` and come on the next visit. Each book then becomes, in this order of checks: a **reread** if its scroll is in the library (quality rises to the new comprehension if higher; hours and tier never change, or rereading would double them); **nothing** if this life already minted it and the scroll was spent or sold (`minted.bin`); **nothing** if it was read for under 30 minutes (anti-farming: marking unread books finished must not mint keys or sellable scrolls); otherwise a **new scroll**. After a successful save, the new hashes go to `minted.bin` and the pending list is replaced by what is left. A failed save leaves both alone, so the same books are minted again next time.
- **Properties:** tier from whole hours read in that book (<3 Mortal, 3-8 Yellow, 8-15 Earth, 15-25 Heaven, 25+ Saint); element from the book's dominant time-of-day bucket (Morning Wood, Afternoon Fire, Evening Metal, Night Water, Earth when none is over 40% or there is no clock data); kind from `contentSeed(world, 60, title hash) % 6`, except that after 4 scrolls without a Cultivation manual the next one is a manual (user); quality from comprehension, which is **where the reader was in the book (book progress %) when it was marked finished** (CrossInk does not record a book's total page count, so "pages read in tracked time" cannot be measured; skimming is already limited by the hours-based tier). Bands: <50 Fragmentary, 50-74 Incomplete, 75-89 Complete, 90-97 Refined, 98+ Perfected.
- **Manual:** one equipped Cultivation manual multiplies the Qi rate by its tier (1.15 / 1.30 / 1.50 / 1.75 / 2.00) x quality (0.90-1.10 over 75-100% comprehension, as `balance_sim.py`). Up to 3 other techniques can be equipped (used by later systems). Equipped scrolls are never spent or sold.
- **Selling:** an unequipped scroll sells for 25 / 60 / 150 / 300 / 500 Spirit Stones by tier (proposed: about a fifth of the stones the book's reading earned). A 65th scroll sells the weakest unequipped one (tier, then comprehension), which may be the new one. Every sale gets a Chronicle line and, for automatic ones, a line in the visit's news.
- **Names:** `{scroll.name} {scroll.kind.<kind>} {scroll.tier.<tier>}` from the packs, themed by element (`~water` ...), with stable picks seeded by `contentSeed(world, 61, title hash)`: the same book gets the same name all life, a different one in another life, and its title never contributes a word. Names are not saved; an SD pack that changes these lists renames scrolls.
- **contentSeed kinds:** 60 kind roll, 61 name, 62 Chronicle lines.

6. **Cultivator:** name with the version ("v0.2.5") on the right, titles (Foundation, Golden Core and Nascent Soul grades, Half-step), realm and stage ("Great Perfection" when entered), Qi bar with the Qi amount and percentage on one line, age and lifespan with Dao Heart on the right, root, talent, Qi rate (with the manual's part) with Spirit Stones on the right, Stamina with HP on the right, consolidation in Great Perfection, origin gifts the one time they are granted, a bottleneck notice (naming the missing key when one is needed), the rumor of the day, a notice when an SD pack has problems, and at the bottom the actions in a two-column grid (v0.2.0, user's choice): Break through, Great Perfection, Techniques, Explore, Alchemy, Meridians (from Qi Condensation, "Meridians (2 ready)" while points can be opened; v0.2.4), Chronicle (during a trial: Tribulation / Heart Demon, and Chronicle). Up/Down walk the cells in reading order. The rumor's room (in the small font) and a missing-key line are reserved first; the talent and then the root line are left out before the rumor is ever cut, and the "no reading" note gives way too.
7. **Breakthrough:** the target realm, the chance and its parts (v0.2.0: the breakthrough pill's), the key scroll that would be used (or the tier that is missing, and the pill that would also open it), the pill the attempt takes, what a failure costs. Attempt rolls; Up/Down picks the pill.
8. **Great Perfection:** what staying at the peak does. Enter enters it.
9. **Outcome:** success or failure, the roll and the chance, the Chronicle line, the Foundation grade when one is formed and the key used up, or the next attempt's chance.
10. **Chronicle:** the Chronicle one page at a time, from the beginning; Next and Previous turn pages, Export saves a copy to `/Chronicles/` (a notice under the page says where), Back returns.
11. **Technique Scrolls** (after the Return summary, or straight after opening the game when a book was finished without new reading): each new, improved, unchanged or sold scroll with its tier, element, kind and quality, and books that gave nothing with the reason. Up to 8 entries over as many pages as needed; more are counted ("N more are in the Chronicle"), automatic sales past 8 get their own total line.
12. **A better manual:** when a new manual beats the equipped one (or none is equipped): the Qi rate now and with it. Equip, or Back to keep things as they are.
13. **Techniques:** the equipped manual and the Qi rate, how many techniques are equipped, then the library (equipped first, then strongest first) with tier, element, kind, quality and tags (equipped, best manual), one page at a time.
14. **A scroll:** name, tier, element, kind, quality and comprehension, hours read, what it does (manual Qi rate, root match) and which breakthroughs it can open as a key; Equip / Unequip and Sell.
15. **Sell:** asks before selling, because a sold scroll's book cannot give another one in this life.
16. **Trial** (v0.1.9): "Strike 2 of 5" or "Scene 1 of 3", what happened on the last one, the strike's or scene's text, HP and its bar with the coming bolt's damage (or the scenes seen through and fallen for), and the answers with their cost (v0.1.10: Raise a Thunder-Warding Talisman, Drink a tonic between strikes, Burn Calm-Heart Incense).
17. **Explore** (v0.1.10): the open regions, each with its danger word and "Charted N / W" or "Cleared · reshapes after N more hours of reading"; then the Bag; then "Use a Region Talisman" when one can be used. Before Qi Condensation it says when the first region opens.
18. **Map:** region name, "Stamina 4 / 20  HP 96 / 120  Charted 10 / 26", the 6 x 6 grid (fog hatched, crags dotted with peaks, an icon per charted tile, a small x on used-up tiles, the player's taiji, dotted frames on the tiles a step can reach, a thick frame on the cursor), then the last event's numbers and line and what the selected tile is. Left/Right move the cursor between your tile and its walkable neighbours; Confirm steps in, trades, takes what has grown back, faces a waiting fight, or opens the Bag.
19. **Fight:** the enemy, its danger word and kind, its line, HP and bar; Fight (chance and cost), Slip away (not from a spirit), Pay them off (bandits), Burn Calm-Heart Incense (spirits) and Drink a tonic when hurt. Back leaves it waiting on the map.
19a. **On the road** (v0.2.2): a met cultivator: name and sect, your rating of them (not for a trade), their line, the HP bar for a challenge; rows: what they offer (Spar / Trade / Accept the challenge, with the chance and the cost), Talk (once), a tonic before a challenge, Walk on. Back leaves it waiting; the map offers it again. Trade opens the Merchant screen; Back there returns here.
20. **Merchant / Sell items:** your stones, the stock with prices (sold-out items say when they return) and Sell items; selling lists the bag with what each fetches. v0.2.1: herbs and ores open **Buy how many** (1, 5, 10, as many as you can).
21. **Bag:** HP and every item held with what it does; Confirm drinks a tonic, takes a grade or Qi Gathering pill, or uses a Region Talisman.
22. **Region Talisman:** which cleared region to reshape.
23. **Alchemy** (v0.2.0): the cauldron (empty, brewing with the reading minutes left, or ready), the last result, then Take out the pills (when ready), Recipes, Experiment and the Bag.
24. **Recipes:** the recipes known, with brew time and how many brews the bag holds parts for. Confirm opens one: what it makes, its parts with what you hold, the brew time and quality roll; Brew starts it.
25. **Experiment:** pick 2 or 3 ingredients (the same one may go in twice), then Light the fire or Start over.
